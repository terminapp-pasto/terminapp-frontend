# Bus photos

- Each company uses its own photo if it exists here, named after the company in
  lowercase with hyphens: `expreso-bolivariano.jpg`, `transipiales.jpg`,
  `transandona.jpg`, `continental-bus.jpg`. No code change is needed.
- `bus.jpg` is the shared photo for companies without their own.
- `.jpg` format, landscape (wider than tall), about 1200 × 500 pixels and under 300 KB.
- Use photos you took or free-license photos (Pexels, Unsplash); company photos
  from their websites and social media are copyrighted.
- If a photo is missing, the page shows a bus illustration in the company's color.
